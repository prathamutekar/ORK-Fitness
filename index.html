About ORK Fitness

Your local gym in Kalher

ORK Fitness is a gym in Kalher, Bhiwandi (Thane), serving people who want a practical place to train for fitness, strength, muscle building, and weight management.

Located on the 1st Floor, B Wing, Hira Arcade Building, Old Agra Road — opposite Evershine English School — ORK Fitness is built around a straightforward local gym experience focused on consistent training.

  • Fitness & general conditioning
  • Strength & muscle building
  • Weight management focused training
  • Convenient location for Kalher & Bhiwandi
